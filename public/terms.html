Terms of play

Last updated 13 September 2026.

The game

Namma Ooru is a free game. The map is illustrated and distances are compressed for play; it is not a navigation map, and landmarks are drawn in a simplified style.

Fair play

  • Choose a friendly nickname. Offensive, hateful or impersonating names are removed.
  • Do not use scripts, bots or modified clients to collect points. The server checks every pickup, and players who cheat can be removed from the leaderboard.
  • Do not click ads repeatedly or ask others to. This harms the game's advertising account.

Chat and voice

  • Be decent to the people you meet. Harassment, hate, threats, sexual content and spam are not allowed, in writing or out loud.
  • Nobody can hear or write to you without you agreeing first. You can refuse, mute or leave a conversation at any moment.
  • Do not record other players, and do not share anybody's personal details, your own included.
  • Report anyone who behaves badly through Feedback in the game. We can mute or ban a player, and we remove chat that breaks these rules.

Leaderboard

Scores and ranks may be adjusted or reset, for example to remove cheating or at the start of a new season.

No warranty

The game is provided as it is, without guarantees that it will always be available or error-free.

Contact

Questions or problems: nestmedia.in/contact, or use Feedback inside the game.
